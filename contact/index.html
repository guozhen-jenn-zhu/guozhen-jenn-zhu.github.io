Contact

Book a time on my calendar or send me a message.

I am always glad to talk about research collaborations in ambient intelligence, foundation models for perception, and multimodal or temporal learning, as well as invited talks and mentoring. The quickest way to reach me is to grab a slot below; otherwise, drop me a note and I will get back to you. I read every message I receive, though replies are sometimes delayed by a few days. If you have not heard back within a week, please feel free to ping me again.
